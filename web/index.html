        Open on your phone

        Keep this server running on your laptop. Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi, then open the laptop’s Wi-Fi IP address with port 8000.

        If you opened this page with localhost, replace localhost with the laptop’s Wi-Fi IPv4 address. Guest or campus Wi-Fi may block connections between devices.
